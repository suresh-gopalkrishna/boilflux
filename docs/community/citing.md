If BoilFlux contributes to your work, please cite the method paper and the software version you used.

**Method**

> S.B. Gopalkrishna, A.K. Nallathambi, E. Specht. Inverse heat conduction method to estimate heat flux
> during quenching of hot metals with infrared thermography measurements. *Applied Thermal Engineering*
> 304 (2026) 132481. doi:[10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2026.132481](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2026.132481)
